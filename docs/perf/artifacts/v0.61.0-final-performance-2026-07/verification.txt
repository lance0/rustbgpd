Verification contract
=====================

Command:
  python3 docs/perf/artifacts/v0.61.0-final-performance-2026-07/verify.py

Expected success:
  PASS: 3 route runs, 71 Criterion rows, sanitization, and checksums verified

Extractor reproduction:
  derive_criterion.py was run against the accepted saved-baseline tree and its
  output compared byte-for-byte with criterion/results.tsv: PASS.

The verifier recomputes every RSS median from the compressed streams, requires
the exact source revision and three-run shape, checks all settled group/member/
fallback/residue/registration/rejection/writer gates, requires four positive
allocator gauges per run, checks raw process status and binary identity, checks
deterministic gzip headers, checks the literal Criterion baseline and clean
swap page counters, validates the pinned 71-name Criterion inventory plus every
median interval and sample count,
rejects mixed-in data from the excluded attempt, scans decompressed content for
identity and runtime residue, and verifies complete checksum coverage.
