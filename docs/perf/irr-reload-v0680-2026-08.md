# IRR reload v0.68.0 refresh — 2026-08-30

This source-equivalent v0.68.0 campaign measured policy reload across one
canonical 320-member IPv4 route-server shape with 183,040 generated prefixes.
The changed policies held 1,000–40,000-prefix IRR lists. Two counterordered
repeats ran four reloads each at 0%, 10%, and 50% received-view overlap.

## Current result

All twelve roots completed. The independent verifier retained all 96 rows,
confirmed 320/320 established sessions and zero parse errors in every row, and
proved the expected received-view delta at each overlap point.

The exact received-view deltas were 0 overlapping member/prefix pairs at 0%,
18,304 at 10%, and 91,520 at 50%. The verifier observed exactly the same
number of suppressed runner-up pairs at each point.

| Overlap | rustbgpd completion p50 range | rustbgpd changed-observer gap p50 range | Grouped control completion p50 range |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0% | 0.852–0.966 s | 399–454 ms | 0.714–0.829 s |
| 10% | 0.876–1.012 s | 457–541 ms | 0.706–0.767 s |
| 50% | 1.030–1.085 s | 631–668 ms | 0.702–0.770 s |

The grouped control is diagnostic, not another daemon comparison.

In the 50%-overlap comparison-A root, BIRD reload 1 retained a changed-observer
gap p95 of 6.015 seconds and maximum of 7.669 seconds, with a 13.995-second
completion p50. The root passed verification; no cause is assigned to this
tail.

At 50% overlap, OpenBGPD's changed-observer gap p50 was 175–273 ms lower than
rustbgpd's in the eight matched repeat/reload pairs. That bounded observer-gap
result does not reverse the completion result: rustbgpd completed each matched
reload in 1.030–1.085 seconds, while OpenBGPD completed in 54.692–61.959
seconds.

## Claim boundary

This result covers one generated 320-member IPv4 route-server configuration on
one host. It does not establish IPv6 behavior, other policy distributions,
larger fleets, live Internet tables, or per-prefix scaling.

The current campaign supersedes the historical multi-second rustbgpd stall
premise for this measured shape. The older receipts remain available as dated
records of earlier implementations and configurations.
